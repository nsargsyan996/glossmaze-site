Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

The short version
  • The game saves your progress on your device only. We don't have servers and we never receive it.
  • There are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to serve and measure ads. You choose whether ads can be personalised.

This policy explains how the Gloss Maze mobile game ("the game", "we", "us") handles information. Gloss Maze is made by Narek Sargsyan, an independent developer who publishes games as Joytap Games. If you have questions, email glossmaze.support@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

To remember where you are, the game saves the following on your phone or tablet:

  • the highest level you have unlocked
  • your star rating for each completed level
  • the coins you have earned in the game
  • the moves you have made in a level you haven't finished, so you can continue it later
  • your settings: ball speed, vibration and language

This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it isn't linked to your identity. Deleting the game deletes it.

We don't use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools of our own, and the game has no sign-in or account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by full-screen ads shown between levels. These ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google, not us, receives the data described here, and Google's use of it is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataWhat it is
IP addressCan be used to estimate your general location (for example, your country or city).
Ad interactionsWhich ads were shown, and whether you tapped or closed them.
DiagnosticsTechnical information about how the game and the ads SDK perform, such as launch time and crashes.
Device and other identifiersYour device's advertising identifier (Android advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking) and similar app or device identifiers.

Google says this data is encrypted in transit. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: when you first open the game, Google's consent form asks whether ads may use your data for personalisation. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings (the gear button), under Privacy settings.
  • On iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track you, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Everywhere: you can manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

The game still works if you decline. You may still see ads, but they won't be personalised using your data.

Children

Gloss Maze is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store personal information ourselves, there is no data about you held by us to access, correct or delete. Your progress can be removed by deleting the game.

For data collected by Google for advertising, Google acts as an independent controller. You can use the choices above, or exercise your rights with Google directly through Google's Privacy Policy. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

We don't sell your personal information.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The playable board on the home page runs entirely in your browser and doesn't store anything. The site uses no cookies, analytics or ads, and its fonts are served from this site rather than a third party. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, for example by adding a new ad partner, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
